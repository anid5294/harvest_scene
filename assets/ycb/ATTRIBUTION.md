The OBJ, MTL, and texture are unmodified. The scene translates the mesh by the
negative bounding-box center `(0.000859, -0.0037845, 0.0355515)` metres without
scaling. MuJoCo renders the textured mesh and uses its convex hull for contact.

Reference: Calli et al., “The YCB Object and Model set: Towards common
benchmarks for manipulation research,” ICAR 2015. DOI: 10.1109/ICAR.2015.7251504.
